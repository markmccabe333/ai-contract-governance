# AI Contract Governance

This repository contains practical templates, exhibits, and reference materials for lawyers negotiating and governing enterprise artificial intelligence (AI) agreements.

The materials focus on issues that arise after deployment—performance degradation, data reuse, retraining, model updates, and other lifecycle risks that are not well addressed by traditional software contracting frameworks.

## Scope

The repository may include:
- Contract exhibits and schedules (e.g., performance specifications, benchmarking datasets)
- Drafting templates for AI-specific provisions
- Practitioner playbooks and checklists
- Reference materials supporting published articles and scholarship

The materials are designed for experienced legal practitioners, including in-house counsel and law firm attorneys working on technology transactions, SaaS, and AI governance.

## Use and Adaptation

These materials are intended as reference tools and starting points. They are not legal advice and must be adapted to the facts, risk profile, and governing law of each transaction.
